## USB 记录与期限 / USB records and deadlines

`core/transport/sensorarraySerialIo.c` 保留唯一物理 TX owner；科学 DATA、stdout/stderr/诊断 LOG、优先 CTRL 分别进入三个有界队列。DATA 与 LOG 各 5 slots/4 entries，CTRL 独立 4 slots/entries；未完成 stdio 行只占 LOG。`sensorarraySerialPublishSlot` 在入队前保存入队时刻与绝对期限：DATA/LOG 250 ms、CTRL 1000 ms。等待、重试、让出均不重置期限。TX 可各持有一个 DATA/LOG/CTRL 候选；整条边界优先 CTRL，然后 DATA，LOG 等待时最多完成 4 条 DATA 就给 LOG 一次机会。正短写先完成当前记录或到期，防止插入其他记录。到期或无效返回保留丢弃/错误计数，必要时先发 delimiter 恢复边界。此轮转不承诺持续 CTRL 洪流下 DATA/LOG 的固定带宽。

`core/transport/sensorarraySerialIo.c` retains one physical TX owner. Scientific DATA, stdout/stderr/diagnostic LOG, and priority CTRL enter three bounded queues. DATA and LOG each have five slots/four entries, and CTRL has four independent slots/entries; incomplete stdio lines occupy LOG only. `sensorarraySerialPublishSlot` records enqueue time and an absolute deadline before publication: 250 ms for DATA/LOG and 1000 ms for CTRL. Waiting, retries and yielding do not restart the deadline. TX can retain one DATA/LOG/CTRL candidate each. Whole-record boundaries prefer CTRL, then DATA, with a waiting LOG receiving a turn after at most four completed DATA records. After a positive short write, the current record completes or expires before another can interleave. Expiry or invalid returns retain drop/error counts and, when required, a delimiter restores the next record boundary. This rotation does not guarantee fixed DATA/LOG bandwidth under continuous CTRL flooding.

已检查并执行 ESP-IDF v5.5.5 的 `usb_serial_jtag_write_bytes` 与 BYTEBUF 相关实现：该驱动同步复制整项或返回 0，BYTEBUF 最大 item 为 ring 大小。TX ring 从 1024 增为 2304 B，容纳最大完整协议记录；每次驱动等待最多 1 tick，剩余不足两 tick 时用 zero-wait，因为 mutex 与 ring 可分别等待。异常正短写仍保守处理，不把驱动 timeout 当 DMA/物理 USB 取消证据。不接管无法确认容量的既有 driver，不私自 remove controller。RTOS deadline 是协作式的：若更高优先级代码使 owner 根本无法调度，清理只能在恢复调度后发生。

The inspected and executed ESP-IDF v5.5.5 `usb_serial_jtag_write_bytes` and BYTEBUF implementation synchronously copy a whole item or return zero; maximum BYTEBUF item size equals ring size. The TX ring grows from 1024 to 2304 B to fit the largest complete protocol record. Each driver wait is at most one tick, with zero wait when fewer than two ticks remain, because mutex and ring may each wait. Unexpected positive short writes still receive conservative handling. A timeout is not evidence of DMA or physical USB cancellation. An existing driver with unknown capacity is not adopted or removed. These are cooperative RTOS deadlines: if higher-priority code prevents the owner from being scheduled, cleanup can only occur after scheduling resumes.

`SERIAL?` 区分完整 driver submission、queue expiry/rejection、正短写、无效驱动返回、yield 和入队年龄；driverAcceptedBytes 是驱动复制字节，**不是 PC 实收**。`serial_classes_v1` 新增 dataSubmitted/logSubmitted/dataRejected/logRejected；前两者是整条 driver 接纳，后两者只计 slots/queue 容量拒绝，不含非法长度调用或超长诊断行。normalSubmitted 仍包括 DATA+LOG，normalExpired 仍为合计；partialWrite 保持“放弃记录已有前缀”的旧语义。USBSTREAM 不扩容、不关闭 RX。物理断连仍可丢整条记录，应用协议仍需完整帧/identity 校验、序号缺口和查询恢复。

`SERIAL?` distinguishes complete driver submission, queue expiry/rejection, positive short writes, invalid driver returns, yields and queue age. driverAcceptedBytes counts copied driver bytes, **not PC reception**. `serial_classes_v1` adds dataSubmitted/logSubmitted/dataRejected/logRejected: the first pair counts complete driver admission, and the second only slot/queue capacity rejection, excluding invalid-size calls and oversized diagnostic lines. normalSubmitted still includes DATA+LOG and normalExpired remains aggregate; partialWrite retains the meaning of an abandoned record with an accepted prefix. USBSTREAM is not enlarged and RX remains active. Physical disconnection may still lose whole records, so complete frame/identity validation, sequence gaps and authoritative queries remain necessary.

CTRL retains an independent 4×512 B, and the driver ring remains 2304 B. Native USB Serial/JTAG is Full-Speed **12 Mbit/s**, as [recorded by this board's sysfs enumeration](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/usb-enumerated-speed-01.json). It is not a UART bridge, and baud-rate settings do not define its wire-throughput limit. The current ESP32-S3 / IDF v5.5.5 path has **no supported Serial/JTAG DMA/GDMA switch**: interrupt-driven CPU copies move bytes from the software ring into endpoint FIFOs of at most 64 B. The separate USB-OTG controller has internal DMA hardware, not a configuration option for current Serial/JTAG. Migration would require proof that the actual driver/stack enables DMA and validation of PHY ownership, enumeration identity and debug coexistence; none is implemented. ## 七任务工作预算与真实 idle / Seven-worker work budget and real idle

本轮新增 `components/sensorarrayWorkBudget`。`d314888` 的 `work_quantum_v2` 将本地时间片和真实 idle backstop 分开：当前 100 Hz 下，每 worker 本地跨度达 **2 ticks（约 20 ms）**或累计 **32 work units**，就在锁外的检查点请求一次 `vTaskDelay(1)`；实际 CPU0 idle 年龄达到 **4 ticks（约 40 ms）**独立触发让出。本地量子在让出后的下一工作边界重开；新真实 idle 也可清本地 burst，但自己的 delay 不更新共享 idle generation。旧两 unit 宽限已取消，兼容 `graceUnits=0`。通知、补队列、成功/失败不续事务 deadline。

The new `components/sensorarrayWorkBudget` separates local quantum from actual-idle backstop in `d314888` / `work_quantum_v2`. At the current 100 Hz, each worker requests one `vTaskDelay(1)` at a checkpoint outside locks after **2 ticks (about 20 ms)** of local work or **32 work units**. An actual CPU0 idle age of **4 ticks (about 40 ms)** independently triggers yielding. The local quantum restarts at the next work boundary after yielding; new actual idle can also clear a local burst, but a worker's own delay does not update shared idle generation. The former two-unit grace is removed, retaining legacy `graceUnits=0`. Notifications, replenishment and success/failure do not renew transaction deadlines.

这针对旧全局 idle 年龄与低优先级调度相位耦合的反复让出，不是永久每记录限速或 Core1 FPS 上限。Hook 注册/DRAM 异常可查询，fallback 只请求一次 tick 延时，不自旋等 idle，不喂 watchdog。screen-04 表明减少让出仍不足以解决 USB 优先级服务问题，不能宣称吞吐修复已验收。实际生产函数的[独立 10 场景量子审阅](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/independent-work-budget-quantum-review.json)与[查询极值实跑](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/quantum-format-01-command.txt)已完成；[旧两 unit 宽限模型](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/independent-work-budget-grace-model.json)仅保留为历史方案。

This targets repeated yielding caused by coupling global idle age to lower-priority scheduling phases, without permanently throttling each record or capping Core1 FPS. Hook/DRAM failures remain queryable; fallback requests only one tick of delay without spinning for idle or feeding the watchdog. Screen-04 shows that fewer yields do not resolve USB priority service, so throughput repair is not accepted. The [independent ten-scenario quantum review](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/independent-work-budget-quantum-review.json) executes production functions, and [query-extrema validation](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/quantum-format-01-command.txt) completed. The [former two-unit grace model](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/independent-work-budget-grace-model.json) remains only as design history.

## PGA 稳定性与运行期 CRC / PGA stability and runtime CRC

`d314888` 另修复 PGA 不稳定分支的 cache 训练错误：三笔新鲜、CRC 通过的样本即使 spread 超限，中位数重复仍可能增加 stableStreak，导致下一组尚不稳定的数据只读一笔便快速接纳。新增九行在该分支清零 stableStreak、饱和递增 unstableStreak，保留诊断历史；新的稳定全样本序列才重新允许快速路径。生产函数的旧版反例与修复后 10 场景已实跑，CRC 拒绝不污染有效 cache/PGA，未提高 spread 阈值。此前 screen-02 的 X12 均在 bypass，不能把此 PGA 缺陷追认为那些实录的根因。见 [PGA 稳定性修复与原失败](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/ads-stability-fix-review.json)和 [repair-08 实跑](artifacts/load_reliability_fix_20261113/repair-08-test_ads_cell_stability.txt)。

Nine added lines reset stableStreak and saturating-increment unstableStreak in that branch while retaining diagnostic history; a new stable full-sample streak must qualify the fast path again. The actual production-function counterexample and ten repaired scenarios were executed. CRC rejection does not train valid caches/PGA, and the spread threshold is unchanged. All screen-02 X12 observations used bypass, so this PGA defect is not retrospectively claimed as their cause. The linked review retains the original failure and repaired test output.

运行期 CRC 风险仍未闭环。历史 `b44c37b` USB-only screen-02 的周期 ADSHEALTH 首末接收跨度 119.612244 s 内，CRC 通过计数增加 140,360、拒绝增加 5,402，拒绝率为 **5,402 / (140,360 + 5,402) = 3.706041%**。分母是已校验 ADC1 数据响应，包含 retry/辅助读取；不能再加 retry，也不是科学点无效率或所有 ADC 产生转换的比例。对应双输出窗口为 1.646187%，不能互相替代。启动每个 PGA/bypass confirm 各 800 笔、各 0 CRC 拒绝，仅证明当时固定 MUX/负载的有限资格，不代表运行期已满足 2.5%。

Runtime CRC risk remains open. In the historical `b44c37b` USB-only screen-02, periodic ADSHEALTH endpoints span 119.612244 s and increase CRC-valid responses by 140,360 and rejections by 5,402: **5,402 / (140,360 + 5,402) = 3.706041%** rejected. The denominator counts checked ADC1 responses, including retry/helper reads; retries must not be added again. This is neither the invalid-scientific-point fraction nor the fraction of all ADC-generated conversions. The corresponding dual-output window is 1.646187%, a different condition. Boot confirmation passed 800 reads in each PGA/bypass profile with zero CRC rejection, establishing only finite qualification at that fixed MUX/load, not runtime compliance with 2.5%.

## 恢复、重启与配置身份 / Recovery, restart and configuration identity

新增 `main/control/sensorarrayRecoveryControl.[ch]`。RECOVER 在 ACK 前占有与 MODE/ROWS/ROWMODES 共用的保留终态槽，ACK 只有本地 control sink 接受成功后才开放执行。请求保存 session/boot/revision/mode/rows/profile 身份；取走前任一域或 known 状态改变，终结 stale_config，不操作新配置。完成时先缓存结果再发布终态，RECOVERY? 无副作用，保留最后结果而非无限历史。旧三个前置 RERR 保留原字段；MACK/RACK 都不等于硬件已应用或主机已接收。

The new `main/control/sensorarrayRecoveryControl.[ch]` reserves the shared MODE/ROWS/ROWMODES terminal slot before ACK. Only local control-sink acceptance opens execution. The request captures session/boot/revision/mode/rows/profile identity; a change in any domain or known status before execution terminates it as stale_config without operating on the newer configuration. Completion caches the result before publication. RECOVERY? is side-effect-free and retains the last result, not unlimited history. The three existing pre-execution RERR cases retain their legacy fields. Neither MACK nor RACK means hardware has applied or the host has received the result.

`main.c` 的 `sensorarrayStopAfterRecoveryFailure` 在预算耗尽或已触及硬件的恢复失败后设置 NONE/DEGRADED、停止 ADS、请求 SAFE，保留 originalError 与首个 cleanupError，并取消未完成的 ADS 自动恢复。真实下一帧入口因此拒绝采样。FullRescue 错误不再被丢弃。前置 busy/stale/不支持配置拒绝不破坏现有合法配置；后续显式 MODE/RESTART 仍可恢复。cleanup 失败时结果不称 route verified；软件 SAFE、GPIO 回读都不代表模拟物理安全，终态明确 physicalSafe=unknown。

In `main.c`, `sensorarrayStopAfterRecoveryFailure` sets NONE/DEGRADED, stops ADS and requests SAFE after restart-budget exhaustion or a recovery failure that touched hardware. It retains originalError and the first cleanupError, and cancels unfinished ADS automatic recovery. The actual next-frame entry then refuses acquisition. FullRescue errors are no longer discarded. Pre-execution busy/stale/unsupported-configuration rejection does not damage a valid current configuration; an explicit later MODE/RESTART may recover. Cleanup failure is not reported as a verified route. Software SAFE and GPIO readback do not establish analog physical safety; terminals explicitly state physicalSafe=unknown.

新增 `sensorarrayRestartControl.[ch]` 使用独立槽，避免普通配置/RECOVER 拥塞永久封锁显式重启。单 pending 不覆盖，未 ACK 或 ACK 本地失败绝不执行；失败缓存可由 RESTART? 查询。显式整机重启意图不因配置 revision 改变而失效，接受/执行时 revision 分开记录。正常、初始化失败和扫描 task 创建失败的三个执行点统一 `sensorarrayPerformManualRestart`，不进入前端硬件锁。执行前缓存 restarting，完整 Serial priority 与网络 Lifecycle 各自提交、有界等待后请求 reset；不宣称 reset 已发生或对端已收到。

The new `sensorarrayRestartControl.[ch]` uses an independent slot so ordinary configuration/RECOVER congestion cannot permanently block explicit restart. A single pending request cannot be overwritten; no ACK or failed local ACK admission means no execution. RESTART? exposes a cached rejection. An explicit whole-device restart is not invalidated by a changed configuration revision; acceptance and execution revisions are recorded separately. Normal operation, initialization failure and scan-task creation failure all use `sensorarrayPerformManualRestart`, without entering frontend hardware locks. It caches restarting before separate complete Serial-priority and network-Lifecycle submissions, then requests reset after a bounded wait. It does not claim that reset occurred or that the peer received the terminal.

RESTART 的 serialQueued/serialError/netQueued/netError 分开记录；网络关闭的 ESP_OK no-op 由新 `sensorarrayTransportPublishLifecycleObserved` 的真实 outQueued=false 识别，不能聚合成假成功。RECOVER 的真实 GuaranteedText drain 本来就显式送 Serial，再独立送 BLE/Wi-Fi；其 terminalQueued 仅代表曾写保留槽。RESTART 的 terminalQueued 只代表至少一个本地输出队列接纳。两者都不是 PC 接收确认。请求 ID 独立域、uint32 回绕跳过 0；新 session 清空缓存，跨 boot 不比较 ID。重启后按 BOOT/CONFIG 新身份重建，不能实现或宣称网络 exactly-once。

RESTART records serialQueued/serialError/netQueued/netError separately. The new `sensorarrayTransportPublishLifecycleObserved` reports actual outQueued=false for the disabled-network ESP_OK no-op, rather than aggregating it into false success. RECOVER's actual GuaranteedText drain already submits explicitly to Serial and then independently to BLE/Wi-Fi; its terminalQueued only means the reserved slot was written. RESTART's terminalQueued means at least one local output queue accepted the record. Neither confirms PC reception. Request IDs belong to separate domains, wrap as uint32 and skip zero. A new session clears caches; IDs are not ordered across boots. Hosts reconstruct from new BOOT/CONFIG identity after reset; network exactly-once is neither implemented nor claimed.
